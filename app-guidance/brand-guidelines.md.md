# ORBIT Brand Guidelines
### by Oaklin

---

## 01 Positioning Statement

> Orbit gives organisations a clear diagnostic of their operational maturity — benchmarked against competitors — so every team knows exactly what's holding them back and what to do next.

---

## 02 Tone of Voice

**Authoritative · Direct · Human**

**Authoritative**
Orbit speaks from evidence. Every insight is grounded in data and benchmarks, never opinion. Users trust what they read because it feels rigorous and objective.

**Direct**
No softening, no jargon, no padding. Orbit tells you what the data shows and what needs to happen next. Respectful but unambiguous — the kind of clarity that saves time.

**Human**
Behind the data is a partner, not a machine. Orbit acknowledges that operational challenges are people challenges. The tone is warm enough to feel supportive without losing its edge.

### Core Principle
Every gap is an opportunity — not a shortcoming. Orbit always frames findings as clear, addressable opportunities. Language is forward-facing: here's where you can go, not where you fell short.

## 04 Typography

**Heading Font:** Arial Bold
Use for all headings, titles, section labels and key callouts.

**Body Font:** Arial Regular
Use for body copy, descriptions, tooltips and supporting text. Arial is a system font — renders cleanly across all environments with no import required.

---

## 05 Visual Do's & Don'ts

### DO

**Lead with data, benchmarks and credibility**
Every finding must be grounded in data and shown in context of industry benchmarks. Where relevant, highlight what high-performing teams do differently — this turns a score into a meaningful, credible insight that clients take seriously.

**Make actions clear, prioritised and time-framed**
Every diagnostic output must connect to a specific action. Clearly distinguish between short-term wins — things a team can act on immediately — and longer-term goals that require sustained effort. Actions should feel purposeful, not generic.

**Keep visuals clean, consistent and focused**
Use colour consistently to signal meaning — the same shade always means the same thing. One insight per visual, never overloaded. White space is intentional. The overall aesthetic should feel reputable and considered, not busy or cluttered.

---

### DON'T

**Never present a finding without context**
A score in isolation means nothing. Always show it against a benchmark or peer average.

**Don't mix action timeframes without labelling them**
Short-term wins and long-term goals must always be clearly distinguished — never grouped together as a flat list.

**Avoid decorative complexity**
No unnecessary gradients, textures or visual noise. Credibility comes from restraint and clarity, not decoration.
