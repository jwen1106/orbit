APP OVERVIEW Name:Orbit

One sentence description: 
An AI-powered diagnostic platform that enables organisations to assess their operational maturity through structured frameworks, translating insights into prioritised short-term tactical actions and longer-term strategic initiatives to help teams become consistently high-performing.
Primary user: 
Client teams within organisations across all industries, spanning back, middle, and front office functions.
Orbit is particularly valuable for organisations that are:
Experiencing high employee turnover
Managing team transitions or restructuring
Adapting to new organisational models
Implementing or stabilising systems and processes
While it is most effective for operational and transactional teams, the platform can also be applied to leadership teams and project-based environments where alignment, performance, and clarity are critical.
Problem it solves: 
Organisations often lack a consistent, objective way to diagnose and prioritise team performance challenges. Issues are typically understood in isolation or through anecdotal feedback, without a structured view across roles or a clear path to resolution.
Orbit addresses this by:
Providing a structured diagnostic approach grounded in operational maturity frameworks
Capturing and comparing perspectives from both team managers and team members
Synthesising inputs into clear, data-driven insights
The platform surfaces the most critical challenges impacting team performance and recommends a tailored set of actions that organisations can implement independently. It also highlights areas where additional support may be beneficial, enabling informed decision-making and identifying potential opportunities for further engagement.
CORE USER JOURNEY
Team members and team managers complete tailored surveys, with question sets adapted to their role and perspective.
The survey is structured around an operational maturity framework, enabling respondents to score each question on a 1–5 scale based on defined criteria.
Upon completion, the team manager gains access to a dashboard summarising aggregated results and key insights.
AI analyses responses across three core high-performing team competencies—People & Relationships, Growth & Impact, and Purpose & Alignment—visualised through a spider diagram to highlight strengths and improvement areas.
The platform generates a prioritised set of short-term tactical actions that teams can implement immediately, alongside longer-term strategic initiatives.
AI provides benchmark comparisons against similar organisations based on industry, team size, and function (back, middle, or front office) to contextualise performance.
An optional insights profile can be completed to help managers understand individual team member preferences, behaviours, and how to maximise their effectiveness.
Oaklin partners with client teams to review outputs, define next steps, and deliver tailored interventions, including a High Performing Teams workshop aligned to the survey results.
FEATURE LIST 
MVP:
Branded survey link with no-login client access
Survey completion and AI question generation
Portfolio Dashboard for leadership view (team manager access only)
Operational Maturity Assessment view
Team Manager / Team Member Analysis
Industry Benchmarking
Action Plan
Post-MVP:
A portfolio dashboard provides client leadership teams with a consolidated view of performance across the three core competencies—People & Relationships, Growth & Impact, and Purpose & Alignment—highlighting key strengths and opportunities.
Users can drill down into the Operational Maturity Assessment to view detailed insights, including spider diagrams, response distributions per question, and AI-generated summaries with key insights, quick wins, and recommended support from Oaklin.
Comparative analysis between team manager and team member responses highlights areas of misalignment, enabling targeted actions to close gaps and improve alignment.
Industry benchmarking compares performance against best-in-class and industry averages, with additional context based on team size and function (back, middle, or front office).
An interactive action plan enables teams to prioritise, track, and measure progress over time, supporting continuous improvement.
Optional features include an Insights Discovery profile to better understand team dynamics, which can be recommended as part of the diagnostic and incorporated into tailored High Performing Teams workshops.
Additional configurable modules include access to case studies, video content, time analysis across core, diverted, and value-add activities, and operational metrics or KPI tracking.
DATA NOTES 
Each team lead sees only their own teams.
Oaklin has a read only and edit access portfolio view across all teams and about to export raw data
Team members do not have access to dashboards or aggregated results.
Client contacts interact solely through the survey link, while team managers receive a separate dashboard link once the survey is closed.
All data is securely scoped within Oaklin, ensuring that no client can access or view another client’s data
